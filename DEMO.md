# Benchmark LMS prototype: 5-minute demo script

**Live URL:** https://davisrideout-sketch.github.io/benchmark-lms-prototype/

Everything is fictional demo data. Nothing is sent anywhere; progress is saved in the browser only. Course content is illustrative sample material, not official Benchmark policy.

**Before you start:** open the link in a normal (not private) window. If anyone has clicked around before, press the circular-arrow **reset** icon in the top bar. First-time visitors see a short **Start here** guide; press **Next** through it (or **Skip**). It can be reopened any time from the **?** / **Start here** button in the top bar.

## 0:00 – Intro (30 sec)
Say: "A clickable preview of a learning platform for Benchmark staff. No real login, so I pick a fictional person. There are three roles: Learner, Manager and Admin."

## 0:30 – Learner journey (about 2 min)
1. On the sign-in screen choose **Learner: James Holloway** (Field Director).
2. **Home** shows his progress, average quiz score, one overdue item and a **Continue learning** card. Press **Continue** on *Winning the First Owner Conversation* (3 of 5 modules done).
3. Read the lesson, then answer the 5-question quiz. Pass mark is 80% (4 of 5). If you miss, the explanations show why; press **Try again**.
4. Pass **Securing the meeting**, press **Next module**, pass the **Knowledge check**, then press **Get your certificate**. Point out **Print / save as PDF**.
5. Quick tour of the left menu: **For Me** (assigned and favourites), **Course Catalogue** (filters), **Knowledge Base** (search), **Video Library**, **Achievements** (the new certificate appears).

## 2:30 – Practice session (about 1.5 min)
1. Still as James, open **Practice Arena** and press **Start scenario** on *Discovery call with a business owner* (about 5 min nominal; the click-through is about 1 min).
2. The fictional owner replies in a chat. Choose a suggested reply, or type your own. A **Coach** note appears after each reply (green is strong, amber is fair, orange needs work).
3. At the end read the **scorecard**: overall score, skills breakdown, strengths and what to improve. Mention the **Practice history** table below on the Practice Arena page.
4. Say: "Replies are scripted in this prototype. The badge says live AI mode is coming; no AI is called."

## 4:00 – Manager reports (about 1 min)
1. Press the **exit icon** (top right) to switch user and choose **Manager: Marcus Delgado**. Note that **Reports** is now in the menu (learners do not have it).
2. **Reports** shows: completion rate, average quiz score, overdue assignments, activity trend, completion by course, and the **Skills heatmap** that flags weak topics (for example Anti-money laundering).
3. Open a name in **Overdue assignments** to drill down to that person's course history. Press **Export CSV** to download the data.
4. Optional, 15 sec: switch to **Admin: Rosalind Okafor** to see the same reports across the whole organisation, plus the **Admin** menu (users, assignments, course statistics).

## 5:00 – Close
Say: "This is demo data only. A real build would add company sign-in (Entra SSO), company hosting, real content and live AI role-play."

## If something goes wrong
- Odd or stuck state: press the **reset** icon, then switch user.
- Mobile: it works on a phone too; open the menu with the three-line icon, top left.
- Lost the guide: press **Start here** in the top bar.

*Review note: this is an internal demo script. Anything shown to others should be reviewed by a person first, and the sample content should not be presented as Benchmark policy.*
